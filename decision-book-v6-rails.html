§3 The Innovative Addition — Trilithoptional bonus day, verified

Studio Tour + Town at Trilith + a Movie

Trilith Studios (Fayetteville, GA — 30–35 minutes south of Atlanta, avoid the 7–9am / 4–7pm windows on I-85) is the largest purpose-built movie studio campus in North America, having hosted productions like Spider-Man: No Way Home and Avengers: Endgame. The public Trilith Studio Tour Experience is a 1.5-hour guided trolley tour — you're seated the whole time, not walking a lot — through two soundstages, real production sets, and prop displays. Book through trilithfoundation.org; ticket price wasn't published, so confirm the current rate directly.

Afterward, Town at Trilith has real dining (CT Cantina & Taqueria among others, indoor and patio seating) and GTC Trilith Cinemas is a full movie theater on-site, open 11am–11pm daily — an easy, seated, air-conditioned finish to the day.

This is genuinely well-suited to a recovery-minded week — trolley, not walking; seated dining; seated cinema — but it's a full addition on top of an already-full week, so treat it as a bonus if there's a Wednesday to spare, not an obligation. Time-box it the same way as everything else: an early-afternoon tour, dinner, and a matinee rather than a late show, so it doesn't eat into anyone's evening recovery window.
