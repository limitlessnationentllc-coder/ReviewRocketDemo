§1 The Route — Stop by StopSat Jul 25 → Wed Jul 29
STOP 1 · SAT JUL 25 · ATL → BLUE RIDGE (90 MIN)

The Railway

Confirmed running $390–660 for six Main attraction

Depart Atlanta ~8:45am. 11:00am boarding, 4-hour / 26-mile round trip along the Toccoa River, with a 2-hour layover in McCaysville, GA — lunch and the one-foot-in-Georgia-one-in-Tennessee photo across the Blue Line. Premier Class (18+, everyone in this party qualifies) gets reclining seats in facing groups of four, on-board restrooms, unlimited soft drinks, a catered snack, and a complimentary group photo — tell the crew it's her 58th. Coach works fine if the budget needs the room; Premier is the nicer ride for the same afternoon.

Check in to the Hampton Inn Blue Ridge — steps from the depot, free parking, free breakfast, rooftop bar with a mountain view for the first toast.

Dinner: Harvest on Main (576 E Main St) — open Sat 11am–3pm lunch, 4–9pm dinner. Blackened Bramlett Farms rainbow trout is the signature plate; grass-fed local steak covers Mom. (706) 946-6164.

STOP 2 · SUN JUL 26 · IN BLUE RIDGE

The Water Stop — replaces Lanier

New water option $84–450 for six

Toccoa River tubing (Tooberoo, McCaysville, ~20 min away) — $14/person for a 1.5-mile, ~90-minute float; life jacket and shuttle included. Low-key, low-cost, low-risk: the whole family can do this one, including anyone who'd skip a boat charter. Or upgrade to a Lake Blue Ridge pontoon (Blue Ridge Boat Rentals or Young Harris Watersports — tri-toons seating up to 14, from roughly $300–450 for a half day) for a proper private-boat afternoon without the Lanier drive or price tag.

Dinner: Grace Prime Steakhouse (55 Mountain St) — open Sun 4–9pm, 45+ day aged steaks. Reserve via Resy or (706) 946-2555. Backup: Black Sheep (closed Mondays, but open Sunday).

STOP 3 · MON JUL 27 · BLUE RIDGE → ATL

The Wind-Down

Travel + dinner$250–320 for six

Checkout, late-morning drive back (90 min), free afternoon to unpack and rest.

Dinner: The Optimist (914 Howell Mill Rd, Westside) — open Mondays 5–10pm (oyster bar from 3pm). Seafood, a scenic patio, and a menu everyone in a six-top can find something on. Reserve ahead; Monday dinner here books up. Atlanta Botanical Garden is closed Mondays — skip it this day, it's on Tue–Sun instead

STOP 4 · TUE JUL 28 · BIRTHDAY · ATLANTA

Her Day — Food Hall

Birthday$100–150 for six

Politan Row (Colony Square, Midtown; open Mon–Thu 11am–10pm) or Ponce City Market (open Tue 10am–9pm) — everyone orders differently, nobody negotiates one menu, and it's low-walking and air-conditioned. Flowers waiting at the table, cake brought in after, cards read aloud — same zero-cost ritual that tested best in the earlier research, just relocated to a casual room instead of a formal one. Cap the night here; no second venue needed.

STOP 5 (OPTIONAL) · WED JUL 29 · DECOMPRESS

Bonus Day

Not required$0–80 for six

If the week can stretch one more day: a slow morning, or pull the standby beach day off the shelf (see §4) for a half-day at Lake Allatoona, 30 minutes out. Otherwise, skip it — the route works as a clean four-day arc without it.

§3 The Train Itselfbrscenic.com · 877-413-8724

Schedule — verified

Summer service runs daily except Wednesday. Departures at 11:00am. This clears up the open question from the earlier chat: Saturday, Sunday, and Monday are all live options, so there's no need to gamble on a Tuesday run — the birthday stays in Atlanta as planned.

Premier vs. Coach

Premier ($100–120/person): 18+ only — every named traveler qualifies — climate-controlled, facing groups of four, unlimited soft drinks, catered snack, group photo.
Coach ($55–75/person): same route and layover, standard seating.

§4 On the Shelf — Beach, Kept as a Standbynot part of this route

Available if wanted, not scheduled

Per your call to cut Lake Lanier and skip the cruise, the beach idea stays available rather than disappearing outright: Red Top Mountain State Park on Lake Allatoona (30 minutes from Atlanta, sand swimming beach, swim hours 7:30am–sunset, parking-only fee) is the nearest real beach day that isn't Lanier. It doesn't fit geographically inside this particular Blue-Ridge-anchored week — it's in the opposite direction — so it's parked here as the answer for a future weekend, or as the Wednesday bonus-day filler in §1 if the trip extends.
