§2 Sunday — Pick the Local AdventureSun Jul 26 · southeast ATL & vicinity

Three real options, ranked by how well they match "cool, close to home, no swimming." Pick one, or read them as a menu — nothing here requires an overnight or a big drive.

Recommended

Beltline Southeast Trail

The Atlanta Beltline's Southeast Trail runs through Grant Park, Cabbagetown, and Reynoldstown — the actual southeast-Atlanta stretch. Walk it, or rent Beltline bikes if legs are tired from Saturday. Stop at New Realm Brewing or Three Taverns Imaginarium for a patio lunch, browse the street art and Ponce City Market's food hall at the north end, and close with dinner in the neighborhood. Flat, flexible, sociable — the closest thing to "cool and easy" on this list.

~$200–370 for six (food/drinks + optional bike rental)
Quieter Alternate

Arabia Mountain & the Monastery

Arabia Mountain National Heritage Area (Lithonia/Conyers, east metro) — a granite-outcrop nature preserve with an easy-to-moderate 2–3 mile walk through hardwood forest and wetlands, ending near the Monastery of the Holy Spirit (grounds open daily 8am–8pm), a genuinely beautiful, peaceful spot for photos. The right pick if Saturday wore everyone out and Sunday should be closer to a rest day than a night out.

Free to walk; ~$100–150 for a casual lunch nearby
Splurge Alternate

Porsche Experience Center

Hapeville, just south of downtown near the airport — a museum, driving-simulator lab, and Restaurant 356 for a genuinely memorable sit-down meal. Track driving experiences run 90 minutes to a full day and are priced per driver (verify current rates directly — not listed publicly); general admission touring and dining don't require it. The "wow" pick if the family wants one properly indulgent afternoon.

Dining ~$250–350 for six; driving time is extra, verify before booking

Optional evening add-on — Stone Mountain Park

Reports have the Music Across America Drone & Light Show running nightly through the end of July over Stone Mountain's lake — a free, sit-and-watch show at dusk, no swimming involved. If Sunday July 26 is confirmed on the schedule (call ahead, this needs verification), it's a low-effort, high-payoff capper to whichever daytime pick you choose. Skip it if everyone's ready for an early night instead.
