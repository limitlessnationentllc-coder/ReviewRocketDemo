Fictional Demonstration

The Keys Changed Hands

Daniel still remembers his father placing the Mustang's keys in his palm. No speech. No ceremony. Just the weight of a familiar set of keys and the realization that the car he had grown up riding in was now his to protect.

His father had bought it off a lot in Detroit in 1971 for $1,400 cash. He drove it home with 34,000 miles on the clock and a dent above the rear wheel arch he never fixed. Said it gave her character. His mother thought the car was trying too hard. She came around. They were married eleven months later. The Mustang was in the wedding photos.

Twenty-eight years after that afternoon in the driveway, Daniel placed those same keys into his own son's hand. No speech. No ceremony. The dent above the rear wheel arch was still there. They keep saying they'll fix it.
